APEX'S MODPACK for Town of Magic  (made for game version 0.77.002)
===================================================================

INSTALL (drag and drop)
  1. Open your Town of Magic folder (the one with TownOfMagic.exe in it).
  2. Drag the "game" and "mods" folders from this zip into it. If Windows asks, choose to merge / replace.
     For HD pictures, also install the HD Pack (every Apex_HD_Pack zip part) the same way.
  3. Start the game. "Apex's Modpack Manager" is at the top of the main menu.

APEX'S MODPACK MANAGER (main menu)
  Switch each mod (and sub-mod) on or off, and use the gear on the right of a mod to change its
  settings (how often Nel taunts, the lust bar per climax, how often the morning scene plays, sound volume...).
  Changes apply after a restart; the manager offers to restart for you.
  It also shows each mod's revision, your game version, the version the pack was made for and the date
  of the last update.

MODS (each one has its own switch in the manager)
  CHARACTERS
    Celica's Autonomy Dildo Events      On quiet mornings Celica sometimes uses the dildo by herself as she wakes (1 in 9).
    H-Scene Idle Loop                   Grope and H-scene animations keep looping on your turn.
    Nel's Auto Battler Sex Autonomy Mod Nel teases monsters during her auto-battles, with a climax ladder.
                                        Balancing options: Half XP and Never One-Shots.
    Nel's Battle Report                 A report sheet when Nel leaves: monsters, items, XP, gold, taunts, time.
  CLOTHING
    Appearance Menu Insta Unlock        Equip > Appearance is open from the start.
    Clothing Remover                    Red-X Shoes / Top / Pants items.
    Combat Helpers                      Green / Orange / Red accessories with extra combat stats.
  ENGINE
    Game Engine Stutter Fix             Removes the short freezes in battle when a pose changes.
    HD x8 Upscale Project (EARLY WIP)   HD pictures when the window is bigger than 1066x600. F8 or the HD Toggle
                                        button (under Health/Magic) switches them on and off.
  UI
    Inventory Scroll Mod                Every item grid (ITEMS, SPELLS, Appearance, Accessories) scrolls with the
                                        mouse wheel and grows as you get more.
    Morden's Lab Craftable Catalog      A CATALOG button in Morden's Lab lists the upgrades you can make now.
    Movable Inventory Items             Drag an item onto another square to swap them (needs Inventory Scroll Mod).
    Inventory Backups                   A backup of your items at each new place; the small button under the
                                        inventory puts one back.

YOUR OWN MODS
  The "mods" folder is for your own mods and mods you download from others. Make one in the manager:
  Mod Tools -> Create a new mod. Full help: mods\_guide\Modding Guide.html.

UNINSTALL
  Delete Town of Magic\game\Apex_Modpack.rpa, game\Apex_HD*.rpa and game\apex_mods_loader.rpe.py.
  The mods folder can stay. Your saves keep working.

GAME UPDATES
  If your game is newer than the version this modpack was made for, every Apex mod is switched off and the main
  menu tells you. To try the modpack anyway, open the manager, set "Test modpack?" to Yes and restart.
  Even then, any mod whose part of the game changed switches itself off and the game runs normally.
  Details are in game\tom_mod_report.txt.
