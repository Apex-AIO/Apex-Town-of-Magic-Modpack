APEX'S HD PACK for Town of Magic (made for game version 0.77.002)
==================================================================

The AI-upscaled HD pictures for the "HD x8 Upscale Project" mod (very early work in progress).
You need Apex's Modpack installed too.
The HD pack can come in several parts (GitHub allows 2 GB per file): install every part.

INSTALL
  Drag the "game" folder from each part's zip into your Town of Magic folder (merge / replace).
  In Apex's Modpack Manager, HD x8 must be on. HD pictures show when the window is bigger than 1066x600.

UNINSTALL
  Delete Town of Magic\game\Apex_HD.rpa and every Apex_HD_<number>.rpa.
